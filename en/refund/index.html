Refund & Subscription Cancellation Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

1. General provisions

The CybLight team (cyblight.org) is dedicated to delivering high-quality digital services and features within CybLight Premium subscriptions (1 Month, 3 Months, 6 Months, 1 Year).

Payment processing and top-ups are securely handled through the Monobank payment infrastructure with support for Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Visa/Mastercard debit/credit cards.

2. 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We provide a 14-day 100% money-back guarantee for initial subscription purchases. If for any reason the service does not meet your expectations, you are entitled to request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase.

Conditions for the 14-day guarantee:

  • The request is submitted within 14 days of the initial payment.
  • Your CybLight account email or username, receipt, or transaction timestamp and amount is provided.

3. Subscription Duration & Renewal

You can enjoy all subscription benefits throughout your entire paid period:

  • Fixed Period: Subscriptions remain active for the exact duration purchased (30, 90, 180, or 365 days). There are zero hidden fees or unexpected recurring auto-debits.
  • Renewal: To extend your plan, simply choose your desired period in your account. The new period seamlessly adds to your existing active time.
  • Data Retention: When a subscription ends, all your device configs and automation setups remain safe in free tier mode.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, please contact our support team:

  • Email: support@cyblight.org with the subject "Refund Request" and your CybLight username.

Refunds are credited back to the original payment card within 3–7 business days according to interbank settlement procedures.

5. Exceptions

Refunds are not granted in the following cases:

  • Requests submitted after 14 calendar days from the initial payment.
  • Account suspension due to severe violations of our Terms of Service (fraud, malicious activities, attacks against services).

6. Contacts

  • Email: support@cyblight.org
  • Contact form: https://cyblight.org/en/contacts/
